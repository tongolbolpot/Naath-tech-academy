Biography & Background

About the Author: Nhial Gach Tut

IT Expert, software developer, author, and passionate community educator.

Nhial Gach Tut

B.Sc. IT & ICT Level IV Professional

Nhial Gach Tut holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Information Technology from University of people and completed ICT Level IV training in Web Development & Database Administration at Mekdela College (Gambella Campus). He serves as a Program Coordinator at Africa Relief Ministries and is the founder of NAATH TECH ACADEMY.

He is also an accomplished author who published the six-chapter book "Nuer Is One Nation" via Amazon KDP, and developed mobile web readers and Amharic-to-Nuer educational platforms.

Naath Tech Academy (Online)

Borderless Digital Literacy Initiative

NAATH TECH ACADEMY delivers flexible, high-quality technical instruction and digital empowerment entirely online. Our reach spans from Bentiu, Fangak, Akobo, Nasir, to students all over the world through virtual community classes and interactive web sessions.

With over 180+ active learners, our online curriculum covers foundational computing, cybersecurity, data analysis, and modern artificial intelligence tools.
